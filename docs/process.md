Tricycle Franchise Application Process
Sangguniang Bayan Staff Workflow
1. Purpose
This document outlines a proposed workflow for Sangguniang Bayan (SB) staff handling requests for tricycle franchise application forms and the subsequent processing of applications. It focuses on tasks performed within the SB office and does not assume the involvement of other Local Government Unit (LGU) offices.
Important: The actual responsibilities, approval authority, required documents, fees, and steps must follow the applicable local ordinance and the municipality's established procedures. Staff should not approve or issue a franchise unless that authority has been formally assigned to them.
2. Process Overview
The process is divided into two distinct stages:
1. Application Form Request — the driver asks for and receives the appropriate form.
2. Franchise Application — the driver submits the completed form and supporting documents for review and processing.
Requesting or receiving a form does not, by itself, mean that a formal franchise application has been submitted.
3. Detailed Staff Workflow
Step 1: Receive the Form Request
When a tricycle driver visits the Sangguniang Bayan office, the staff member should:
- Ask whether the request concerns a new franchise, renewal, or another type of application.
- Identify the correct application form based on the request.
- Explain the basic instructions for completing the form.
- Inform the driver of the applicable requirements and submission procedure, based on current local rules.
Suggested status: Form Requested → Form Issued
Step 2: Issue the Application Form
The staff member provides the correct form to the driver.
Where appropriate, the staff member may record:
- Date of request
- Applicant's name and contact information, if collected under office procedure
- Type of request (new franchise, renewal, or other)
- Date the form was issued
- Name or identifier of the staff member who issued it
The staff member should explain where and how the completed form must be returned.
Suggested status: Form Issued
Step 3: Receive the Completed Application
When the driver returns, the staff member receives the completed application form and supporting documents.
The staff member should:
- Check that the required sections of the form have been completed.
- Check that the documents required by local rules have been included.
- Provide an acknowledgment of receipt if required by office procedure.
- Tell the applicant about any missing or unclear items.
Possible supporting documents may include identification, vehicle registration documents, a driver's license, proof of ownership or authorization to use the vehicle, and other locally required documents. The official checklist must be based on the municipality's current requirements.
Suggested status: Submitted
Step 4: Verify the Application and Documents
The staff member conducts an initial completeness and consistency check.
The review may include:
- Confirming that required fields are completed.
- Checking that names and vehicle details are consistent across the submitted documents.
- Confirming that required supporting documents are present and legible.
- Identifying missing, expired, inconsistent, or unclear information that needs follow-up.
If requirements are incomplete, the staff member should record the deficiencies and inform the applicant what must be corrected or submitted. The application should not be marked as complete until the required items have been provided.
Suggested statuses: Under Verification, For Compliance, or Verified
Step 5: Register and Track the Application
Once the application is received, the staff member records it in the official logbook or application management system, following office procedures.
Recommended record fields include:
- Application reference number
- Applicant's name and contact information, where appropriate
- Application type
- Tricycle and vehicle details
- Date the application was received
- Checklist of documents submitted
- Assigned staff member
- Current application status
- Notes on deficiencies, follow-ups, or actions taken
The system should distinguish a form request from a formally submitted application. It should also maintain a record of status changes and the staff member responsible for each update.
Suggested status: Application Recorded
Step 6: Prepare and Endorse the Application
After the initial review is complete, the staff member organizes the application and supporting documents for the next authorized stage.
Depending on local procedures, this may involve:
- Preparing an application summary or checklist.
- Organizing the supporting documents.
- Preparing an endorsement or transmittal record.
- Forwarding the application to the authorized SB officer, committee, or other decision-making body, where required.
- Recording the date and recipient of the endorsement.
The staff member should not treat an application as approved merely because the documents are complete or the application has been endorsed.
Suggested status: For Endorsement or For Approval
Step 7: Record the Official Decision
When an official decision is received, the staff member records the outcome in the application record.
Possible outcomes include:
- Approved — the application has received the required formal approval.
- Rejected — the application has been formally denied; record the stated reason when available.
- For Compliance — additional documents or corrections are required.
- Pending — a decision has not yet been made.
The record should identify the decision date and the relevant approving authority or official record, in accordance with office procedures.
Suggested status: Approved, Rejected, For Compliance, or Pending
Step 8: Prepare and Record Document Release
For an approved application, the authorized staff member prepares the applicable franchise certificate, permit, or other official document for release, subject to all required conditions.
The staff member should:
- Verify that the required approval and conditions have been satisfied.
- Confirm the details printed on the document.
- Record the franchise or permit number, validity period, and expiration date, where applicable.
- Record the release date and the recipient.
- Obtain an acknowledgment or signature of receipt if required by office procedure.
If the application is not approved, the staff member should follow the official notification and recordkeeping procedure instead of preparing an approval document.
Suggested statuses: Ready for Release, Released, or Completed
4. Recommended Application Statuses
Status	Meaning
Form Requested	The driver has asked for an application form.
Form Issued	The correct form has been provided to the driver.
Submitted	The completed form and supporting documents have been submitted.
Under Verification	Staff are checking the form and documents.
For Compliance	The applicant must submit missing information or correct deficiencies.
Verified	The initial document review is complete.
Application Recorded	The application has been entered into the official record.
For Endorsement	The application is being prepared or forwarded for the next authorized stage.
For Approval	The application is awaiting the required formal decision.
Approved	The application has been formally approved.
Rejected	The application has been formally denied.
Ready for Release	The approved document is ready to be released, subject to applicable conditions.
Released	The document has been released to the applicant.
Completed	The required processing and recordkeeping steps have been completed.


Not every municipality will use every status. The final status list should match the actual local procedure.
5. Recommended System Modules
Module	Purpose
Form Requests	Records requests for application forms before formal submission.
Franchise Applications	Stores submitted applications and applicant/vehicle details.
Document Verification	Tracks required documents, deficiencies, and verification results.
Application Tracking	Shows current status, dates, assigned staff, and follow-up actions.
Endorsements and Decisions	Records endorsements and official decisions.
Franchise Records	Maintains records of approved franchises and relevant details.
Document Release	Records issued documents, release dates, and acknowledgment of receipt.
Audit Trail	Records important changes, including who changed a status and when.


6. Basic Controls and Recordkeeping
To support accurate and accountable processing, the system or office procedure should:
- Restrict application approval and document issuance to authorized personnel.
- Keep form requests separate from submitted applications.
- Record who received, reviewed, updated, endorsed, or released an application.
- Keep a history of status changes and important actions.
- Limit access to applicant information to staff who need it for their duties.
- Use an official checklist of requirements approved for the municipality.
- Avoid recording an application as approved until the required formal decision is documented.
7. Summary Workflow
Form Requested → Form Issued → Application Submitted → Under Verification → For Compliance, if needed → Verified → Application Recorded → For Endorsement / Approval → Official Decision → Ready for Release → Released / Completed
The workflow above is a general operational model. Before implementation, confirm the actual process, requirements, responsible personnel, and approval authority under the municipality's applicable ordinance and procedures.

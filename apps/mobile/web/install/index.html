Android app

Install the SBTF app on your phone

The official app for tricycle drivers and operators of Mabini, Batangas. File a franchise application, upload your requirements, follow the review, and keep your certificate — wherever you are.

  • Android 7.0 or newer
  • Free
  • Works on mobile data

Scan with your phone camera

Point the camera at the code, then tap the link that appears.

sbtf-application.vercel.app/install

How to install

  1. Scan the QR code

    Use your phone camera. If you are reading this on the phone already, just tap Download.

  2. Tap Download

    Choose “Download for Android”. If your browser warns about the file type, choose “Download anyway”.

  3. Allow the install

    Android will ask permission to install from your browser. Open Settings, allow “Install unknown apps” for that browser, then tap the downloaded file.

  4. Open SBTF and sign in

    Create an account with your e-mail address, or sign in if you already registered. A 6-digit code sent to your e-mail confirms the account.

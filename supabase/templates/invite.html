Kung hindi gumagana ang button, kopyahin ang link na ito sa iyong browser:

Ano ang susunod na mangyayari

1. Magtakda ka ng sarili mong password.

2. Dadalhin ka sa sign-in screen ng console.

3. Kailangang aprubahan ng administrator ang account bago mo ito magamit. Hanggang sa panahong iyon, lalabas ang “awaiting approval”.

Gumagana ang link na ito nang isang beses lamang at may limitadong oras. Kung na-expire ito, humingi ng bagong imbitasyon sa municipal administrator.

Good day,

A municipal administrator of Mabini, Batangas created a staff account for you in the SBTF System. Use the button below to set your password.

Set my password  →

If the button does not work, copy this link into your browser:

{{ .SiteURL }}/auth/confirm?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&type=invite

What happens next

1. Choose your own password.

2. You are taken to the console sign-in screen.

3. A municipal administrator must approve the account before you can use the console. Until then, signing in shows “awaiting approval”.

This link works only once and expires after a limited time. If it has expired, ask the municipal administrator for a new invitation.
